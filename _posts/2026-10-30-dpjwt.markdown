As enterprises move from experimenting with AI to deploying AI agents in day-to-day operations, a familiar challenge reappears: how do we let these agents interact with systems, data, and applications in a secure and controlled way?

We have seen this pattern before. When the internet became widely adopted, firewalls became essential. When organizations began exposing services, API gateways became the standard way to manage and secure access. Today a similar shift is underway. As AI agents proliferate, the AI gateway is emerging as the control point that governs how they interact with the rest of the organization.
